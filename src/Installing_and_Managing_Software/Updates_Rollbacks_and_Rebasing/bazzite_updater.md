# Bazzite Updater

![Bazzite Updater|1703x1137, 50%](/img/bazzite-updater.png)

!!! info "Bazzite Updater is added with the release of Bazzite 44!"

The new **Bazzite Updater**, made by [Robert French](https://github.com/rfrench3), allows update, rollback, and viewing changelogs on a Graphical UI, and can be used entirely with a controller!

---

## Usage

### Updating

Simply press the update button. You may also view its progress by pressing the **Toggle Console** button in the **top right corner**.

---

### Viewing Changelogs

![Bazzite Updater Changelogs|1683x1137, 50%](/img/bazzite-updater-changelogs.png)

Changelogs can be viewed by clicking the **Changelogs** entry under the hamburger menu in the **top left corner**.

!!! tip "Make sure to take note of your **current image version** and branch when viewing. A lot of releases are for the **testing** branch only."

---

### Rollback Updates

![Bazzite Updater Utilities|1751x1163, 50%](/img/bazzite-updater-utilities.png)

Rolling back updates can be done by clicking the **Other Utilities** entry under the hamburger menu in the **top left corner**.

---

## Adding Bazzite Updater to Steam

![Add Bazzite Updater to Steam|897x612, 50%](/img/bazzite-updater-add-to-steam.png)

In [Bazzite Portal](/Installing_and_Managing_Software/Bazzite_Portal.md), Select **Manage Bazzite → Update your system → Add Bazzite Updater to Steam Big Picture**.
